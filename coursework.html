University of Calabria

MSc, 2024 – 2026

Wireless Devices and Networks

From the radio channel to short-range and vehicle networks: how wireless devices share the air.
Radio foundations
Path loss, multipath, shadowing and the Okumura–Hata model; FDMA, TDMA, CDMA and OFDMA.
Wi-Fi
DSSS and OFDM physical layers, CSMA/CA, RTS/CTS, and the DCF and PCF access functions.
Wi-Fi quality of service and security
IEEE 802.11e (EDCA and HCCA), the weaknesses of WEP, then WPA, WPA2 and 802.1X.
Bluetooth
Classic, Low Energy and Mesh; frequency hopping, piconets, scatternets and power modes.
RFID
Passive and active tags, anti-collision methods and EPCglobal.
Mesh and ad hoc networks
IEEE 802.11s, the ETX and WCETT routing metrics, and location-aided routing. Related: ad hoc routing protocols and ZigBee.
Vehicle communication
V2X with IEEE 802.11p, 802.11bd and Cellular-V2X.

Networking Aspects of Internet of Things

How small devices communicate, from low-power radio links to application protocols.
Short range
IEEE 802.15.4 (CSMA/CA, reliability, parameter tuning), time-slotted channel hopping in 802.15.4e, and ZigBee. Related: Bluetooth.
Long range
LoRa chirp spread spectrum; LoRaWAN architecture, device classes, activation and security.
IPv6 for small devices
6LoWPAN header compression and fragmentation.
Application layer
CoAP messages, reliability, requests and responses.
Sensor network transport
  • RMST
  • PSFQ
  • ESRT
Routing and medium access
Covered with Wireless Sensor Networks.

Distributed Systems and Cloud/Edge Computing

Where computation should happen: from the central cloud to the network edge.
Distributed systems and IoT
Centralized, decentralized and distributed designs; interoperability, scalability and autonomy.
IoT development
Methodologies, frameworks, platforms and tools, from analysis to deployment.
Cloud, fog and edge
Architectures and the trade-offs between delay, bandwidth, energy and privacy.
Multi-access edge computing
Its link with 5G, the ETSI architecture, host and system levels, and reference points.
Simulation
EdgeCloudSim: mobility, network and load models, and task orchestration.

Simulation and Performance Optimization

How to model systems with random behavior, simulate them and choose the best design.
Queueing models
Producer–consumer station, open and closed systems, central server model; waiting time, queue length, throughput and utilization.
Probability and stochastic processes
Joint and marginal distributions, the Erlang distribution, stationarity and Poisson arrivals.
Simulation studies
Model design, input data, verification, validation and experiment design.
Arena
Flowchart and data modules, resources, queues and reports.
Output analysis
Chi-square goodness-of-fit test; confidence intervals from independent replications and batch means.
Simulation-based optimization
Ranking and selection, and subset selection.

Related to my research interest in digital twins.

Latakia University

BSc, 2018 – 2024

Information Theory and Coding

How to measure information, how much a channel can carry, and how to compress data.
Measuring information
Self-information, entropy, redundancy, information rate, conditional and joint entropy, mutual information.
Channels
Noiseless, noisy and memoryless channels, the binary symmetric channel and the noise matrix.
Capacity
The Shannon–Hartley law, the ideal system, and trading bandwidth against signal-to-noise ratio.
Coding
Source and channel coding, lossless and lossy compression, average code length and efficiency.
Compression algorithms
  • Shannon–Fano
  • Static Huffman
  • Adaptive Huffman
  • LZ77
  • LZ78

Antennas

How antennas radiate, and how their shape and arrangement direct the beam.
Foundations
Frequency bands, antenna types, radiation mechanism, current distribution and input impedance.
Parameters
Radiation pattern, beamwidth, radiation intensity, effective length and area, noise temperature and field regions.
Linear antennas
Short, finite-length and half-wave dipoles, monopoles and image theory, traveling-wave antennas.
Arrays
Array factor; broadside, end-fire and phased (scanning) arrays; directivity.
Practical designs
Helical antennas (normal and axial modes), Yagi–Uda and the folded dipole.

Advanced Cellular Systems

From GSM to 3G radio access and packet data, with an introduction to 5G New Radio.
GSM and its evolution
Radio, switching and operation subsystems; FDMA and TDMA; GPRS and EDGE.
Radio access
FDD and TDD, CDMA, direct-sequence spread spectrum and WCDMA codes.
UMTS architecture and channels
User equipment, Node B, RNC and core network; logical, transport and physical channels.
Procedures
GPRS attach, PDP context activation, routing area update and traffic classes.
5G services and radio
Enhanced broadband, ultra-reliable low-latency and massive machine communication; spectrum, scalable OFDM, frame structure, mini-slots, bandwidth parts, massive MIMO and beamforming.
5G architecture
Standalone and non-standalone deployment, service-based core, network slicing, Cloud-RAN and virtualization.
Protocols
  • RRC
  • PDCP
  • RLC
  • MAC
  • NBAP
  • RANAP
  • GMM
  • SM
  • GTP
  • MAP
  • SCTP
MATLAB course labs
CDMA with Walsh codes, scrambling, fading channels, and BCH coding with interleaving.

Wireless Sensor Networks

From the sensing element to the network: how sensor nodes measure, communicate and save energy.
Sensors and transducers
Potentiometers, encoders, LVDT and RVDT, torque, piezoelectric, strain gauge, pressure, flow and temperature sensors.
Foundations
Node architecture, topologies, deployment and applications; underground, underwater, multimedia and body networks.
Communication
The protocol stack, IEEE 802.15.4 and ZigBee, and IEEE 802.11.
Routing protocols
  • SPIN
  • Directed diffusion
  • LEACH
  • PEGASIS
  • TEEN
  • GAF
  • GEAR

Also multipath, query-based and quality-of-service routing.

Energy-saving protocols
  • S-MAC
  • T-MAC
  • DMAC
  • PAMAS
  • STEM

With duty cycling, data reduction and mobile sinks.

Data aggregation
Tree-based, cluster-based and secure aggregation.

Network Design and Planning

Routing in networks without fixed infrastructure, and software-defined networking in practice.
Ad hoc networks
Mobile (MANET), vehicle (VANET), flying (FANET) and sensor networks.
Proactive routing
  • DSDV
  • OLSR
  • WRP
  • FSR
Reactive routing
  • AODV
  • DSR
  • TORA
Hybrid routing
  • ZRP
  • ZHLS
Software-defined networking
Separate control and data planes, with northbound and southbound interfaces.
Course labs
Topologies in Mininet and MiniEdit with Python; OpenFlow messages in Wireshark; flow tables with ovs-ofctl; the Floodlight REST API, firewall and access control lists.
Performance measurement
Delay and throughput with ping and iperf, controller tests with cbench, plots in gnuplot.

Further topics

Machine Learning

How to learn from network data: predict, classify, detect anomalies and make decisions.
Method
Data exploration and preparation, training and test sets, cross-validation and avoiding data leakage.
Regression
Linear and polynomial models, the bias–variance trade-off, regularization, feature scaling and selection.
Classification
Logistic and softmax regression, decision trees, class imbalance, precision, recall and F1 score.
Neural networks
Multilayer perceptrons, backpropagation, the Adam optimizer and control of overfitting.
Clustering and anomaly detection
K-means, silhouette score, isolation forests, autoencoders and ROC curves.
Reinforcement learning
Markov decision processes, Q-learning, exploration and function approximation.
Black-box optimization
Surrogate models, Latin hypercube sampling, kriging and expected improvement.
Connections
Decision trees reuse entropy from information theory. Optimizing costly functions also appears in simulation.
