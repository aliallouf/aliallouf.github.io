Spatial traffic anomaly detection with optimal transport

Research pipeline

Compares how traffic is distributed across sensors using Wasserstein (Earth Mover's) distance, with spatiotemporal cost matrices, to flag deviations from expected behaviour.

Wasserstein and sliced Wasserstein distance, Python, Madrid Traffic Portal data

From traffic data to verifiable behaviour models

Formal modelling

Decision trees divide traffic behaviour into 44 temporal windows, and clustering their outputs limits state explosion. The model is expressed as ReqIF requirements, a SysML v2.0 state machine and SMT-LIB2 constraints checked with the Z3 solver, with what-if scenarios traced back to requirements.

Decision trees, clustering, Z3, SysML v2.0, UPPAAL, ReqIF
